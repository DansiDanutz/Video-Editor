# Veo 3.1 pilot — three era shots for "Did You Know That?" No. 05 · Food

Purpose: test whether AI-generated b-roll (Google AI Ultra → Flow / Veo 3.1) lifts the episode look without
breaking the channel's neon-on-navy identity. Three shots, one per era, generated by David in Flow with Ultra
credits (no API). I composite them in Remotion under the same audit gates, and we compare side by side with the
current illustrated version before deciding anything for the series.

## Settings in Flow (same for all three)
- Model: Veo 3.1 · Duration 8 s · Aspect 16:9 · 1080p (upscale to 4K is not needed; the master is 1080p30).
- Generate 2 variants per prompt (6 clips total); I pick the best per shot.
- Sound: ignore Veo's audio — the clips are muted; Brian, our SFX and the bed stay as they are.
- Download as MP4 into `~/Downloads/DYK-05-Veo-pilot/` named `1500-a.mp4`, `1500-b.mp4`, `1747-a.mp4` … (any
  naming is fine, I'll sort them).

## The style lock (paste at the end of every prompt)
> Cinematic, photoreal, period-accurate. Low-key lighting with a deep navy-blue shadow tone; one warm practical light
> source in frame; a subtle cyan or magenta rim light on edges. Shallow depth of field, 35 mm look, slow steady camera
> move, no fast motion. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no modern objects, no identifiable faces (faces
> turned away, in shadow or out of focus). Lower third of the frame and the top-left corner kept free of important
> detail. Fine film grain. 24 fps feel.

(Why: captions sit in the lower third, the episode HUD sits top-left, and the "HOW WE SAW IT" chip docks top-right.
Faces are avoided so no real person is implied — this also keeps the AI-use answer on YouTube "No".)

**Prompt**

> camera, tends the oven in soft focus.

**What I need from it:** the first 6 s clean (the "Did you know that…" card lands at ~6 s and takes the right half).
